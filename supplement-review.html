Magnesium Bisglycinate

Chelated magnesium · Minerals · Sleep, stress & muscle function

About the ingredient

Magnesium bisglycinate (magnesium glycinate) is a chelate in which one magnesium ion is bound to two molecules of the amino acid glycine. The chelated form is absorbed well and is generally gentle on the gut, unlike magnesium oxide or citrate.

Magnesium supports hundreds of enzymatic reactions, including muscle and nerve function, energy metabolism and sleep regulation. Glycine itself has a calming effect on the nervous system.

Positives

  • High bioavailability compared with magnesium oxide
  • Gentle on the stomach, with a low laxative effect
  • May improve sleep quality and reduce subjective stress
  • Helps with muscle cramps and recovery when intake is low
  • Glycine adds a mild calming effect

Negatives

  • More expensive per mg of elemental magnesium than oxide or citrate
  • Low elemental Mg per capsule, so several capsules per dose
  • Direct clinical evidence for the glycinate form is still limited
  • Caution in kidney disease and with some medications (antibiotics, bisphosphonates)
